Delete your SabkiJeb account

You can delete your account in the app, or ask JOSH Studio to help if you cannot sign in.

In the app

  1. Open SabkiJeb and go to Profile.
  2. Choose Delete account. Update the app if this option is missing.
  3. Read the confirmation, then choose Delete Account.

Deletion happens at once and cannot be undone. Unsent changes on your phone are lost too; let them sync first if you want them kept in shared records.

If you cannot sign in

Email support@sabkijeb.app with the phone number you sign in with and ask to delete your account. We will confirm the number is yours and complete deletion within 30 days. Never email an OTP, PIN or bank details.

What is removed

Your sign-in and sessions are deleted. Your profile becomes “Deleted user”, with your phone number and UPI ID removed. Your friends list is deleted.

What stays

Shared expenses, loans, repayments, settle-ups, dispute reasons and activity history stay in the other people’s records, including names and phone numbers they entered. Your anonymised profile stays attached so their totals still add up. Deleting an account does not cancel a debt.

Server logs and any provider-held backups may remain until they expire on the provider’s schedule. Read the privacy policy’s deletion details for the full retention information.
