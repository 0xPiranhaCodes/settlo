What SabkiJeb is

SabkiJeb is a record-keeping app. You can record shared expenses in a space (a flat, a trip, a group of friends), see who owes whom, and record personal loans between two people with their repayments.

  • Not a bank, payment service or lender. SabkiJeb does not hold, send, receive or lend money, and never sees your bank account, card or UPI PIN.
  • Payments happen in your UPI app. When you tap to pay, SabkiJeb opens your own UPI app with the details filled in. You approve the payment there. What happens in that app is between you, your bank and that app’s provider.
  • What records show. Balances and statements reflect what members enter and confirm. SabkiJeb doesn't independently verify payments, and doesn't itself create or determine an enforceable debt.
  • Loans are between you and the other person. SabkiJeb only records the terms you enter. We do not set, check or enforce interest, schedules or repayment, and we do not collect money for anyone.

Shared records

SabkiJeb is built for records that more than one person keeps together.

  • Members of a space see its members (names and phone numbers), expenses, settle-ups and activity history. The two people in a loan see the loan and its repayments.
  • Entries you add, edit, confirm or dispute, including any reason you type, are visible to those people, and every change is kept in the activity history.
  • Deleting an expense or a space hides it and records the deletion; it is not erased from our servers.
  • If you delete your account, the expenses, loans, repayments, settle-ups and history you share with others stay in their records, including the name and phone number they entered for you. Your profile is shown as “Deleted user”.
