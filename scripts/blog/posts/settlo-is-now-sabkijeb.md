Settlo is now SabkiJeb. You'll see a new name, a new logo and a palette of jade, forest green and marigold, along with our website at sabkijeb.app.

The idea stays simple: keep shared expenses and personal loans clear between people who know each other.

## Your records stay with you

Nothing about your data changes because of the rebrand. Your spaces, expenses, loans and payment records remain yours to use as before. You do not need to start again or create a new account for the new name.

SabkiJeb still records what people enter and confirm. Money moves in your own UPI app; a new logo does not change that boundary.

## A familiar place, with a new address

The website brings together a guide to the app, our [privacy policy](/privacy/), [Terms of use](/terms/) and [support](/support/). JOSH Studio remains the operator, and you can reach us at [support@sabkijeb.app](mailto:support@sabkijeb.app).

We're still in the iPhone TestFlight beta. Android is planned for later. If you'd like to try SabkiJeb, [email us about beta access](mailto:support@sabkijeb.app?subject=SabkiJeb%20TestFlight%20beta); an enquiry is not an automatic invitation.

Good friends. Clear balances. Now with a name for everyone's pocket.
