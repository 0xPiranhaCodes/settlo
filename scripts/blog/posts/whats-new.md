A short record of what's live in SabkiJeb. We'll add dated entries here as more features ship, so you can see what changed without reading a roadmap as a promise.

## 13 November 2026

- **A redesigned website.** sabkijeb.app now explains Spaces, shared expenses, personal loans and paying through your own UPI app, with real app screens. It also brings support and account information together.
- **This blog.** Product updates and practical guides, starting with [how to split bills with friends](/blog/split-bills-with-friends/).
- **Terms of use.** The [Terms](/terms/) explain the service and the role of JOSH Studio, which operates SabkiJeb. SabkiJeb is a record-keeping app, not a bank, payment service or lender.

## 12 November 2026

- **Settlo is now SabkiJeb.** The app has a new name and logo. The rebrand does not change your existing data. Read the [announcement](/blog/settlo-is-now-sabkijeb/).
- **A redesigned palette.** Jade, forest green and marigold bring the new identity into the app and website, with light and dark themes.
- **Account deletion information.** Open Profile in the app and choose Delete account. If you cannot sign in, email support@sabkijeb.app. Deletion takes effect at once and cannot be undone; records shared with other people stay in their records. The [deletion page](/delete-account/) and [privacy policy](/privacy/#deletion) explain what is removed and what remains.

## Availability

SabkiJeb is free to use and currently available through the iPhone TestFlight beta. Android is planned for later; there is no public App Store or Google Play release yet. [Email us about beta access](mailto:support@sabkijeb.app?subject=SabkiJeb%20TestFlight%20beta) if you'd like to try it.
