# Fair share is the member's agreed split

**Date:** 9 Nov 2026 · **Decided by:** manager (tie-break on PR #24) · **Owner review:** optional

**Question.** In `space_monthly_fairness` and the Space › balances bars (canvas note n12), is a member's fair share an equal 1/n of the month's spend, or the sum of their splits?

**Decision.** The sum of the member's agreed splits for the month. An exact, percent or shares split is the group's own definition of fair; an equal tick would show someone who pays exactly what was agreed as over- or under-paying. For equal splits the two coincide, including the rule that remainder paise go to the payer.

**Consequence.** The white tick on the balances bar sits at the member's agreed share. If the owner wants an equal-share view as well, it is a separate toggle, not a change to this view.
