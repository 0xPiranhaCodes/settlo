# Design audit, 14 Nov 2026 (#279)

**Result: 17 Pass, 13 Awkward, 1 Broken across 31 scored checkpoints.** The Broken checkpoint is the inline repayment controls' target size and AX2 clipping, already covered by **#272 P5**; no duplicate issue is needed. The largest remaining changes are already assigned to #278: sheet presentation/chrome, inset groups, tab titles and glass close controls. This PR changes documentation and captured evidence only.

These are checkpoint counts, not counts of screens or a WCAG conformance claim. Each checkpoint appears once in the scorecard; a screen can exercise several checkpoints. Pass means the stated check passed within the recorded coverage. Awkward means usable but inconsistent with the specified guideline. Broken means a required control loses information or fails the explicit accessibility requirement.

## How this was tested

- **Pinned baseline:** `origin/main` fetched at the start of the run, `6a928eb1b177598c1cf1cecd4ebfae40cfa2e324` (merge of #275, space navigation). All app captures use that source. **#277 merged during the audit**; F3 is baseline evidence of behavior that its changes address, not a claim that the newly merged fix regresses. #278 was not awaited or applied.
- **Build:** `cd app && flutter build ios --simulator --debug -t integration_test/palette_app.dart`. Signed-out build adds `--dart-define=PALETTE_AUTH=true`. Both succeeded; logs are in [evidence](../evidence/279/README.md).
- **Owned devices only:** iPhone 16, **393×852 pt**, iOS 26.1 (`01772457-16CE-49DD-9504-50BA8F8A148D`); iPhone SE (3rd generation), **375×667 pt**, iOS 26.1 (`782D61AF-F435-46DB-9C40-FFE0011C1C29`). Installation initially stalled on the fresh devices; only the audit's iPhone 16 was restarted. Cleanup is recorded with the evidence.
- **Conditions:** system light/dark; `simctl ui content_size large` and `accessibility-large` (AX2, about 1.94× body text, the issue's approximate “200%” setting). This is not an injected exact 2.000× Flutter scaler. Root tabs have captures in all four appearance/text combinations on both devices. Other flows use the targeted matrix below, not every permutation.
- **Input:** physical-coordinate HID taps and drags via the repository's `docs/evidence/188/simtouch.m`, compiled in worktree scratch. No XCUITest label-based tap substitutes. Screenshots are simulator framebuffer captures, not goldens or generated illustrations.
- **System settings:** Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion were checked independently on iPhone 16; there is also a combined dark capture. A small UIKit probe records the actual `UIAccessibility…Enabled()` values. Merely writing `ReduceTransparencyEnabled` did **not** enable the setting; the real Settings switch did, setting `EnhancedBackgroundContrastEnabled`. This false start was not treated as a passing accessibility check.
- **Checks:** core token contrast suite **91 passed**; pressable/glass/liquid-glass suites **19 passed**. These prove their specific token/widget contracts, not full screen-reader operation. Live motion recording exposed a dismissal issue despite those passing widget checks.
- **Fixture limits:** repositories are the committed palette fakes. No SMS, production account deletion, invite delivery, WhatsApp message, payment or live backend mutation was performed. The synthetic typed sign-in number was `+91 90000 00001`, then masked in published captures. Existing profile numbers, phone hints and invite suffixes were also masked. OTP-to-session changes, UPI return, cross-repository consistency and haptics on hardware are not established by these fixtures.
- **Accessibility limit:** icon/field labels and semantic structure were source-reviewed; full VoiceOver/Full Keyboard Access traversal was **not** exercised. Focus order therefore remains unverified and is not included as a Pass. Screenshots do not prove spoken order or focus restoration. Device settings were exercised on the iPhone 16; the SE coverage concentrates on layout/text rather than duplicating every settings permutation.

## Guideline clauses

- **HIG:** [Layout](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout), [Typography](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography), [Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility), [Sheets](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets), [Tab bars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars), [Lists and tables](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/lists-and-tables), [Materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials). References retrieved 14 Nov 2026. Apple's guidance informs the audit; #278's exact 16 pt margins, 26 pt groups and sheet-everywhere decisions are owner specifications, not universal HIG mandates.
- **Liquid Glass:** [Apple overview](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technologyoverviews/liquid-glass) and Materials: native controls, separation of content and controls, restrained tint, and adaptation to accessibility settings.
- **Local contract:** [ui-guidelines.md](../ui-guidelines.md), especially Platform, Spacing, Glass, Accessibility, Feedback and Small screens. #278 is the future target where it intentionally changes this document's existing 20 pt margin / left-close pattern.
- **WCAG 2.2:** [1.4.3 Contrast](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-minimum), [1.4.4 Resize text](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#resize-text), [1.4.11 Non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#non-text-contrast), [2.4.3 Focus order](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#focus-order), [2.5.8 Target size (minimum)](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#target-size-minimum). WCAG AA's target minimum is **24 CSS px with exceptions**; Settlo's **44×44 pt** contract is stricter. The 36 pt pill is not automatically a 2.5.8 failure, but its lost text is relevant to 1.4.4 and its target fails the local rule. Token contrast tests do not measure every composited glass pixel or disabled control.

## Fix plan and exclusions

1. **#272 P5:** fix the Broken repayment action before treating AX2 as complete. Existing acceptance already covers target size, edge taps and large text.
2. **#278 phase 1:** implement the shared sheet/header/groups; add explicit Reduce Transparency propagation and a real Reduce Motion dismissal recording to acceptance.
3. **#278 phase 2:** migrate secondary screens and Profile; remove Home's decorative hero/mark emphasis and unify routine action tint.
4. **#278 phase 3 / #261 / #272:** spacing, icon scaling, participant entry, Shares defaults, keypad action, statement title and OTP helper wrapping.
5. **Follow-up verification:** repeat full VoiceOver/Full Keyboard Access focus traversal, hardware haptics, and live-backend completion separately. They are not silently counted as passing in this report.

No new Broken finding lies outside the existing #272 scope, so **zero new defect issues** were filed. The Awkward checklist is attached to #278.
